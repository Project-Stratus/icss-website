High Powered Rocketry (new!)

Design and build a model rocket on a G-class motor and get it as high as possible. Bonus points for collecting data on the way up and getting it back in one piece.

ICSS used to do this until 2023 and we're bringing it back. We've already got the equipment, supplies and funding, and we've done the National Rocketry Championship before so we'd like to go back.

You'd pick up aerodynamics, electronics, structures, OpenRocket, MATLAB, CAD/FEA/CFD, laser cutting and 3D printing.

You don't need any experience, it's a new low-risk project so don't worry about breaking things. Meeting times and rooms get posted in the rocketry group chat in the ICSS WhatsApp community.
