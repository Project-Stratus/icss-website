What it is

Design and build a high-powered model rocket on a G-class motor. Main aim: the highest possible apogee. Side quests: collect data during the flight with on-board sensors, and get the rocket back in one piece.

ICSS ran this project until 2023 and we’re bringing it back. The equipment, supplies and funding are ready to go, and we have pedigree in the UK National Rocketry Championship.

MotorG-class
GoalMaximum apogee + flight data + recovery
Experience neededNone! Low-risk, new project — don’t worry about making mistakes

Skills & techniques you’ll pick up

  • aerodynamics
  • OpenRocket
  • MATLAB
  • CAD
  • FEA
  • CFD
  • laser cutting
  • 3D printing
  • electronics & flight sensors
  • structures & mechanisms
  • recovery systems
