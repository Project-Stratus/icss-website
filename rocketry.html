High Powered Rocketry (new!)

The aim of this project is to design and build a high-powered model rocket fitted with a G-class motor, and get it as high as possible. Alongside that we want to collect data during the flight using on-board sensors, and (ideally) recover the rocket in one piece.

ICSS ran this project until 2023 and we're now bringing it back - we already have the equipment, supplies and funding ready to go. We have some pedigree in the National Rocketry Championship (NRC), so we'd love to get back to entering it more consistently, and give members the chance to get involved with a rocketry project at its grassroots.

What you'll learn

The project covers a bit of everything: aerodynamics, electronics, mechanics, structures and mechanisms. You'll get experience with OpenRocket, MATLAB, CAD, FEA and CFD, as well as manufacturing through laser cutting, 3D printing and assembly.

You don't need any relevant experience - this is a new, fairly low-risk project, so don't worry about making mistakes. Meeting times and rooms get posted in the rocketry group chat in the ICSS WhatsApp community.
