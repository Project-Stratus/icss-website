Project Svarog

An interstellar solar sailing CubeSat

Project Svarog is a student designed, built and tested CubeSat. The long-term aim is to get it to the edge of the solar system using a solar sail, which no student team has done before.

What we did last year

Over the past year we've built on our flight heritage from the BEXUS balloon campaign in Sweden, and developed and tested a number of satellite subsystems:

  • prototype carbon-composite deployable booms
  • time-of-flight sensors to measure the sail deployment
  • a complete, working solar sail orbital propagation and simulation codebase
  • a technical demonstration and proof of concept for the future Low Earth Orbit mission, flown on BEXUS-36

This year

With these subsystems demonstrated on BEXUS-34 and BEXUS-36, the team has now started on Phase 0 of the Low Earth Orbit demonstrator mission. This year will focus on taking it from an initial mission concept to a preliminary design, while also building a FlatSat flight prototype.

New for this year is a composites engineer role, responsible for designing the new composite booms for the sails.

We take a freshers-first approach - we run projects to teach new members how to design, build and test satellite systems, and you'll be given responsibility for a real part of the design and manufacturing process, with us there to guide you through it. Hopefully some of you'll be the ones who eventually see it launch. There are also trips to ESA for testing.
