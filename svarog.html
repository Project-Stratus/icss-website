What it is

A student designed, student built, student tested CubeSat. The aim: reach the edge of the Solar System on a solar sail — and be the first student team to do it.

Timeline so far: project inception (2021) → vacuum chamber testing (2023) → balloon tests on BEXUS (2024/25) → Low Earth Orbit test (2026) → interstellar launch (2030?).

PlatformCubeSat with a deployable solar sail
Flown onBEXUS stratospheric balloon campaign (×2), Esrange, Sweden
Next milestoneSail deployment test in Low Earth Orbit
Sub-teamsElectronics · Orbital sims · Mechanical design + testing · Structural sims · Plasma modelling · Outreach / media

Skills & techniques you’ll pick up

  • PCB design
  • electronics box manufacture
  • orbital simulation
  • orbital optimisation
  • structural simulation
  • energy momentum method
  • plasma modelling
  • composite booms
  • mechanical design
  • vacuum-chamber & drop testing
  • integration & assembly
  • academic writing

  • Multiple trips to ESA for testing
