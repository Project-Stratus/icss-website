Join us!

If you know nothing we’ll teach you — if you know loads you’ll teach us.

step_1.exe×

1. Buy a membership — £5 for the year. It unlocks every project, talk and event.

[ buy membership ]

Membership is through Imperial College Union, so you’ll need to be a Union member.

step_2.exe×

2. Join the WhatsApp community. Every project has a group chat in there, and it’s where meeting times, rooms and events get posted.

[ ask for the WhatsApp link ]

step_3.exe×

3. Turn up. Pick a project (or three) and come to its intro session. No applications, no experience needed.
