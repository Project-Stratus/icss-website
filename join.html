Join us

Four steps, and none of them needs an application.

1. Buy a membership - it's £5 for the year and gives you access to all of the projects, talks and events.
Buy membership

2. Join the WhatsApp community. Each project has its own group chat in there, and it's where we post meeting times and events.
Join the WhatsApp community

3. Come to the Welcome Talk on Tuesday 6 October, 18:00-20:00 in RSM 131, where every project explains what it does this year.

4. Pick a project (or a few) and come along. Each one runs an intro course at the start of the year, so you don't need to know anything yet.
