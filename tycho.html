Project Tycho

A high-thrust liquid bipropellant rocket engine

what it is · skills · pictures · updates · achievements · join

What it is

Tycho is a team of around 20 undergraduate, masters and PhD students designing, building and firing a liquid rocket engine. It started life as Project Kepler; Tycho is the next-generation design.

Two goals: break the UK student record for the highest thrust produced by a liquid bipropellant engine (10 kN), and become the first student team to test a liquid bipropellant engine with turbomachinery designed in-house.

PropellantsLiquid oxygen + isopropyl alcohol (IPA)
Thrust / chamber pressure~11 kN (~1,120 kgf) at 60 bar
Power cycleGas-generator (open) cycle
InjectorCoaxial swirl
CoolingLOx regenerative + film cooling
AlsoSupersonic turbomachinery, autogenous re-pressurisation

Skills & techniques you’ll pick up

  • CAD
  • FEA / stress analysis
  • CFD
  • regenerative cooling analysis
  • turbomachinery design
  • injector design
  • nozzle design
  • machining
  • additive manufacturing (metal AM)
  • propulsion analysis software
  • flow control electronics
  • pressure transducers
  • test procedures & safety
  • design reviews (PDR / CDR)

Key achievements

  • Hot-fired at Race 2 Space (Airborne Engineering test bays, Westcott Venture Park) — 4,500 N of thrust, enough to lift a grand piano
  • Turbine testing (as part of a final-year project) confirmed a highly effective design
  • Wrote and passed the CDR and safety / testing procedures
  • New integrated chamber + nozzle, new injector, turbomachinery manufactured
  • Tycho thrust chamber PDR submitted to Race 2 Space
