Launch watch parties

When there's a big launch on, we book a room and put it up on the big screen. Next up is a watch party for the Rocket Factory Augsburg launch, with the date set by the launch itself. Launches get delayed a lot, so to keep us busy while we wait there's usually a space quiz running as well.

Hackathons

This term's hackathon is Lunar Logistics on 14-15 November: in teams of 4 you design how to set up and grow a permanent settlement on the Moon, then present it. It's design and presentation only, with pizza lunch both days. Last year we ran two hardware hackathons:

  • LEOHack - simulating the in-space docking of satellites (sponsored by Magdrive)
  • SatHack - simulating autonomous space debris avoidance (sponsored by Lodestar Space)

Each one has hardware, software and presentation tasks, with access to 3D printers, circuit assembly tools and prototyping space. Lunch was provided, and over £300 in prizes was given out.

Other things we run

  • Movie nights (with free snacks)
  • Trivia nights, sometimes with AstroSoc
  • Industry talks - for example from ESA and the Science Museum
  • Trips, such as the tour of RAL Space

We also help out with the UK Space Design Competition, UKROC and CanSat, through hosting, volunteering and giving talks.

Dates get posted in the WhatsApp community first, so it's worth joining.
