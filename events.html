Launch watch parties

When there's a big launch on we get a room and put it on the big screen. Launches get delayed a lot so there's usually a space quiz going while we wait.

Hackathons

Last year we ran two:

  • LEOHack: simulating satellites docking in space (sponsored by Magdrive)
  • SatHack: simulating autonomous space debris avoidance (sponsored by Lodestar Space)

Hardware, software and presentation tasks, 3D printers and circuit stuff to use, free lunch, and over £300 in prizes. The next one is on 21-22 November.

Other stuff we do

  • Movie nights (free snacks)
  • Trivia nights, sometimes with AstroSoc
  • Industry talks, e.g. the Science Museum's Head of Space, ESA's head of recruitment
  • Trips, like the RAL Space tour

We also help out with the UK Space Design Competition, UKROC and CanSat.

Dates get posted in the WhatsApp community first.
