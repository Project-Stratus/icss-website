What it is

Design and build a Mars-style rover that tackles realistic challenges in mobility, autonomy and planetary exploration, and compete in the UKSEDS Olympus Rover Trials. This year it’s a joint ICSS × IPRL entry.

Low-stakes and accessible: no prior rover experience needed, and a great way to pick up technical and project-management experience.

CompetitionUKSEDS Olympus Rover Trials
TeamICSS × IPRL joint entry
Experience neededNone

Skills & techniques you’ll pick up

  • mobility & suspension
  • autonomy
  • mechanical design
  • electronics
  • rapid prototyping
  • project management
