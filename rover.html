Mini Rover (new!)

A joint ICSS x IPRL team building a small Mars-style rover for the UKSEDS Olympus Rover Trials. Mobility, autonomy, the usual rover problems.

Last year IPRL built an entry in about a month on not much money, so there's loads of room to make it better. No rover experience needed, and it's a good way to get some project management experience too.

Meeting times and rooms get posted in the rover group chat in the ICSS WhatsApp community.
