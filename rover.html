Mini Rover (new!)

This project will surround the design of a Mars-style rover developed to tackle realistic challenges in mobility, autonomy and planetary exploration, all in order to compete in the Olympus Rover Trials!

This project will be supported by the strong expertise built up by the Imperial Planetary Robotics Lab, but is designed for some relative newcomers to come along and try their hand at end-to-end rover design and project management for the first time.

Meeting times and rooms get posted in the rover group chat in the ICSS WhatsApp community.
