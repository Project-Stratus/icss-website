Autumn Hackathon 2026: Lunar Logistics

WhenSaturday 14 and Sunday 15 November 2026
WhereImperial College London, South Kensington (room to be confirmed)
Teams4 people. Bring your own team or we'll match you with one.
CostFree

The challenge

Starting from where the space sector is today, design a complete logistics strategy for a permanent settlement on the Moon: what gets launched, when, how it gets there and how the base is kept supplied. It's all design and presentation, so there's no hardware to build, and there are prizes for the best plans.

Signing up

Sign-ups open soon and close on Saturday 7 November. The link goes out in the WhatsApp community first, so join that to hear about it.
