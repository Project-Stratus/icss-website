Another Year, Another AGM

February 2, 2015 · posted by guilds_icseds

from the archive

Our 2015 AGM will be held at 6pm on Thursday 19th March in Room 301 of the Skempton (Civil Engineering) Buildin’.

We give you a round-up of the year, and then you elect a new committee.

Interested in running for election? All positions are open to any ICSEDS member in any year, and are guaranteed* to enrich your time at Imperial. Nominate yourself here, or drop us a line (icseds@ic.ac.uk) if you’d like to know more about a position and the work it entails.

*ICSEDS accepts no responsibility for the veracity of this claim.
