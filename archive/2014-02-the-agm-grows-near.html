The AGM Grows Near

February 13, 2014 · posted by Joseph Dudley

from the archive

Our AGM will be held at 6pm on Thursday 13th March in Lecture Theatre 2.28 of the Royal School of Mines.

We give you a round-up of the year, and then you elect a new committee.

Interested in running for election? All positions are open to any student in any year, and are guaranteed* to enrich your time at Imperial. Drop us a line if you’d like to know more about a position and the work it entails.

*ICSEDS accepts no responsibility for the veracity of this claim.
