IPRL 2023 – New Partner!

May 30, 2023 · posted by Ivan Revenga Riesco

from the archive

It is said that the devil is in the details. An aspect of engineering that is often overlooked is the way large components are assembled and bolted together – indeed it is most likely a component will fail at its connection to other subassemblies. 

Therefore we are very grateful to have partnered with Accu, who will be providing us with precision components such as fasteners and bearings! These will be installed on the Harbinger rover in the upcoming weeks. 

Accu is a world-renowned UK-based supplier of precision components for all kinds of engineering projects. They supply parts to over 50% of the world’s top 100 manufacturers while also supporting smaller projects (a nod at student teams) – all reaching out to over 125 countries. 

So, if you want to finally turn your project into reality, head over to their website
