A Successful Launch

October 10, 2013 · posted by Joseph Dudley

from the archive

Great to see so many people at today’s meeting, we’re just hoping we’ll have enough rockets!

Full details of all our projects can be found here. You’ll need to buy membership and project tickets on the Union website.

Ballooning will start next Tuesday (15th) at 6pm in Skempton 064A
Rocketry will start next Thursday (17th) at 6pm in Skempton 301
Engine Design will start on Monday 21st at 6pm in Skempton 060A

If you missed the meeting, don’t worry, just turn up to whichever project you want to be involved in, our drop us an email at icseds@imperial.ac.uk.

Don’t forget that the excellent people at AstroSoc also run space events, so you should check our their website too.

If you haven’t already, make sure you sign up to our mailing list, like us on Facebook, or follow us on Twitter so that you don’t miss any updates.

Have a great weekend!

Joseph
