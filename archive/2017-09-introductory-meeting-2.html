Introductory Meeting

September 5, 2017 · posted by guilds_icseds

from the archive

A new year full of rockets and space is about to start! Come along to our introductory event on the 10th of October in Skempton 301 to enjoy the company of space-minded people (there might also be free food 😉 ). If you have any questions that you did not ask at the Freshers’ Fair, this will be the time to ask. Project officers will also be there to present the projects that will run through the year.
