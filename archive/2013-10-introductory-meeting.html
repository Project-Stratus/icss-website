Introductory Meeting on Thursday 10th

October 4, 2013 · posted by Joseph Dudley

from the archive

Lectures may have reappeared on your calendar, but so has ICSEDS! Our introductory meeting will be on Thursday 10th October at 6pm. Contrary to what it says on our leaflets, it will not be in Skempton 164, but in the Royal School of Mines LT 1.47 (first floor on the right).

You’ll get to know more about our rocketry, ballooning and other projects, and meet the committee. If you came to us hoping for Astronomy, then don’t despair, our good friends in AstroSoc will be very happy to have you. If you can’t make it, don’t worry, just drop us an email at icseds@imperial.ac.uk and let us know which projects you want to get involved in.

If you haven’t already, make sure you sign up to our mailing list, like us on Facebook, or follow us on Twitter so that you don’t miss any updates.

If you’re feeling really keen, you can become a member, which you can do quickly and cheaply on the Union website.

I look forward to seeing you on the 10th.

Joseph
ICSEDS Vice-Chair
