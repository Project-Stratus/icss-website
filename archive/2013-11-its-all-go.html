It’s all go!

November 26, 2013 · posted by Joseph Dudley

from the archive

It’s all go here at ICSEDS mission control! The model rocketeers have finished building their first kit rockets are now using OpenRocket to design their own; the high powered rocketry team is carefully crafting the parts in needs; the balloonists are finalising their designs before they purchase materials; and the engine design group is making great progress having been inspired by a talk from Kingston University’s Adam Baker.

Outside of our projects we’ve had a great trip to the Science Museum at the end of last month, and have are off to see the aclaimed Gravity this weekend. Currently we’re busy planning an exciting event for next month so stay tuned.

We’ve also ordered out mission patches which are set to arrive for Christmas, and acquired a storage area courtesy of the Aeronautics department. Now all we need is some sponsorship!
