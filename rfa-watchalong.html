RFA launch watch party

Rocket Factory Augsburg (RFA) is getting ready for the first orbital launch from SaxaVord Spaceport in Shetland. When it goes, we'll be watching it on the big screen on campus.

WhenThe launch date isn't set yet. We book a room as soon as a launch window is announced and post the date in the WhatsApp community.
WhereImperial College London, South Kensington (room confirmed with the date)
CostFree for ICSS members (join here)

Launches slip a lot, so there'll be snacks and a space quiz to keep us busy while we wait. If there's still no launch window by late November, we'll run a launch replay night instead.
