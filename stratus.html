Project Stratus

High-altitude ballooning, steered by AI

what it is · skills · pictures · updates · achievements · join

What it is

Stratus builds and flies high-altitude weather balloons fitted with release valves, ballast and an AI agent. By rising and sinking to catch winds that blow in different directions at different altitudes, the balloon can hold its position (within ~100 km of launch) — and come home instead of bursting.

The result: a cheap, versatile alternative to low-orbit satellites for weather monitoring and Earth observation.

Altitude~30 km, −50 °C, <1% of sea-level pressure
ControlReinforcement-learning agent, vertical control only
SensorsTemperature, barometer, battery monitor, GPS (I²C) · microSD, LoRaWAN (serial) · UV (analogue)
Also on boardCamera, power bank, insulated container, parachute
ApplicationDetecting “ship tracks” to support fuel-sulphur regulation

Skills & techniques you’ll pick up

  • reinforcement learning
  • atmospheric simulation
  • embedded electronics
  • Raspberry Pi Pico
  • I²C / serial / analogue sensors
  • LoRaWAN radio
  • GPS
  • flight-path prediction
  • valve & ballast design
  • lathe, waterjet & TIG welding
  • soldering
  • launch logistics
