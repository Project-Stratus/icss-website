What it is

IPRL is a student-led initiative, run with the Imperial College Robotics Society (ICRS), with one mission: give students the space to gain experience in space robotics — rovers, drones, even mini robot swarms.

It has three branches: Harbinger, the rover team competing in the European Rover Challenge; Triton, the research branch; and the Alumni Project. More are on the way.

CompetitionEuropean Rover Challenge (ERC), Kraków
ERC tasksMaintenance · Probing · Navigation (rover + drone) · Science · Presentation
Rover sub-teamsElectronics · Drive / chassis / suspension · Robotic arm · Drill / payload · Science · UAV
Research (Triton)Applications · Geoscience · Engineering challenges
CommitmentRover team 4–8 hrs/week · Research 3–6 hrs/week

Skills & techniques you’ll pick up

  • robotic arm design
  • drive, chassis & suspension
  • electronics
  • UAV autonomy
  • autonomous navigation
  • ArUco marker localisation
  • drilling & sample payloads
  • astrobiology
  • research writing
  • project management
