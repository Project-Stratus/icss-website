Projects

All our projects are run by students and most don't need any experience. Every project runs an intro course at the start of the year so you can turn up knowing nothing (Tycho's is a 4 week rocket engine design course, for example).

Tycho

Liquid bipropellant rocket engine, going for the UK student thrust record. Hot-fired at 4.5 kN last July.

Svarog

Solar sailing CubeSat that wants to get to the edge of the solar system. Flown on BEXUS twice.

Stratus

High altitude weather balloons that steer themselves using reinforcement learning.

IPRL

Imperial Planetary Robotics Lab. Rovers, drones and planetary research. 16th at the European Rover Challenge 2025.

High Powered Rocketry (new!)

G-class model rockets, back after a couple of years off.

Mini Rover (new!)

Joint ICSS x IPRL rover for the UKSEDS Olympus Rover Trials.
