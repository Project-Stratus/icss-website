Good to know×

Every project runs an intro course at the start of the year, so you can turn up knowing nothing. Tycho, for example, runs a 4-week Rocket Engine Design Crash Course where you design your own engine with the same software as the real one.

Meeting times are posted in each project’s group chat in the ICSS WhatsApp community.

Cultivating enthusiasm for space engineering and science.
If you know nothing we’ll teach you — if you know loads you’ll teach us!
