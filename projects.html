Projects

All of our projects are entirely student led, and most don't require any prior experience. Every project also runs some form of introductory course or task at the start of the year, so it's completely fine to turn up knowing nothing (Tycho's, for example, is a 4 week rocket engine design course).

Tycho

A liquid bipropellant rocket engine, aiming for the UK student thrust record. Hot-fired at 4.5 kN at Race 2 Space last July.

Svarog

An interstellar solar sailing CubeSat. Having flown on BEXUS-34 and BEXUS-36, the team is now working on a Low Earth Orbit demonstrator.

Stratus

High altitude weather balloons that use reinforcement learning to steer themselves by changing altitude.

IPRL

The Imperial Planetary Robotics Lab, covering rovers, drones and planetary research. Placed 16th at the European Rover Challenge 2025.

High Powered Rocketry (new!)

A high-powered model rocket on a G-class motor, back after a couple of years away.

Mini Rover (new!)

A joint ICSS and IPRL rover for the UKSEDS Olympus Rover Trials, aimed at relative newcomers.
