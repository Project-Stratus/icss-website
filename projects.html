Projects

All of our projects are entirely student led, and most don't need any prior experience. Every project runs an intro course or task at the start of the year, so it's fine to turn up knowing nothing.

Tycho

A liquid bipropellant rocket engine, aiming for the UK student thrust record. Hot-fired at 4.5 kN at Race 2 Space.

Thursdays 18:00 · no experience needed

Svarog

An interstellar solar sailing CubeSat. After flights on BEXUS-34 and BEXUS-36, the team is working on a Low Earth Orbit demonstrator.

Mondays 18:00 · freshers first

Stratus

High altitude balloons that use reinforcement learning to steer themselves by changing altitude. Last flew 7 February 2026.

Fridays 18:00 · about one launch a term

IPRL

The Imperial Planetary Robotics Lab: a European Rover Challenge team, drones and planetary research. 16th at ERC 2025.

Rover team Wednesdays 14:00 · 4-8 h a week

High Powered Rocketry

Design, build and fly G-class rockets with our own flight computer. Last flew to 721 m in 2023, and restarting this year.

Weekly 18:00 · no experience needed

Mini Rover

A joint ICSS and IPRL rover for the UKSEDS Olympus Rover Trials, built for newcomers.
